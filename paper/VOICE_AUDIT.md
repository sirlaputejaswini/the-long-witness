# Voice audit — "Records That Outlive Their Systems"

Checked against the author's standing bars: plain, specific, human;
no stock AI phrasing; no inflated significance claims; failures printed
next to wins; measured vs modeled labeled; constructions disclosed.

## Slop scan (phrases searched, all absent from the .tex)

"delve", "moreover", "furthermore", "it is worth noting", "in today's
fast-paced", "landscape" (as metaphor), "leverage" (as verb),
"cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "game-changer", "seamless",
"harness the power", "in conclusion" (as a crutch opener), "plays a
crucial role", "ever-evolving", "tapestry". Em-dash count is moderate
and used as punctuation, not decoration.

## Human-voice anchors kept deliberately

- The introduction opens on one true scene (02:13, Nov 4, 2010) told
  from the record itself, not on a field-level generality.
- Sentences a person would say out loud: "A bare 'unlock' is an
  anecdote"; "Endings are quieter than beginnings"; "It does not
  flatter. It stays."
- Numbers are given with their denominators throughout E5, including
  the weak ones, in the author's established pattern.

## Honesty placements

- Blind spot (reforged truncation, 0/5) appears in the abstract, E3,
  Section V, and the README — four places, same wording strength.
- Door anchoring weakness (0.190) is reported with the reason, and the
  text explicitly declines to redefine the check to flatter the method.
- Ground-truth-by-construction disclosure: Section V, paragraph 2
  (device layer constructed; decision stream a benchmark's; sensor
  stream measured).
- Scripted-policies limitation: Sections III and V.
- Context-not-re-derived limitation: Section V.
- Tiered summarization is described as *designed, not measured* in
  Section V, and appears as patentable subject matter rather than as
  a result.

## Flags for the author's own pass (not blockers)

1. Abstract length (~300 words) — IEEE journal abstracts often run
   shorter; author's call.
2. The conclusion's final two sentences are the most rhetorical in
   the paper. They are hers in substance (the witness framing is her
   naming), but she may want to read them aloud once.
3. Section VI (patentable subject matter) is deliberately plain
   engineering disclosure; patent counsel should review before any
   public posting, per the standing patent-sequencing practice.
